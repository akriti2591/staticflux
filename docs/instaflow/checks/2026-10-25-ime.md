## Findings for the harness / app
1. CJK text is invisible until "Use local CJK fonts" is clicked (only Latin/Arabic/Devanagari/Thai fonts bundled) and missing glyphs render as nothing, not tofu. Real app should ship/load CJK fonts or draw .notdef boxes. Must be re-clicked after every reload.
2. EditContext mode only: setControlBounds height grows by ~one line (38 px) on each "key" intent during composition (38 → 76 → 114 → 152 → 173 → 211 → 249) although no newline is inserted. Does not affect candidate placement. Hidden-textarea mode stayed at 38 px.
3. The "different words" in steps 4–6 came from the macOS Japanese IME's predictive candidates (日本語版, 日本語字幕) / kana revert, not from the page.
